We're excited to have you join our community of independent beauty and wellness professionals. Below is everything you need to complete before your move-in date. Work through each item and reach out if you have any questions.

To Complete
  • Pay your deposit as outlined in your agreement.
  • Upload your required documents (Driver's License, Professional License, Liability Insurance Certificate, ACH Form) via the Document Upload Form.
  • Complete your member application (link will be sent separately).
  • Obtain liability insurance. Not sure where to start? Browse our recommended insurance vendors here.
  • Sign your lease via DocuSign (you'll receive a separate DocuSign email to complete this step).
What to Review
  • Community Handbook: policies, shared space guidelines, and what to expect as a member.
  • Virtual Leasing Folder: your lease, suite details, and important documents in one place.
  • Handyman service requests: for any maintenance needs after move-in, contact Stacey Cleveland directly at 612-429-6360.
Preparing for Move-In

After your lease is signed and documents are received, you'll get two separate emails: one with your My Salon Suite app login and one with your ButterflyMX access credentials. Both are required to access your suite on move-in day.
