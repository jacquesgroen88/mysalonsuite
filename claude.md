# Project Constitution: My Salon Suite - Blaine

## Behavioral Rules & Invariants
- **Primary Goal**: Maximize "Book a Tour" conversions.
- **Mindset**: "A stylist lands here from a Google Ad and decides in 10–20 seconds if this is worth booking."
- **Design Aesthetic**: Premium, clean, aspirational. Apple-level spacing, Airbnb-style lifestyle imagery, high-end beauty brand aesthetic.
- **Avoid**: Corporate stiffness, overloaded text, generic stock feel, generic agency-style fluff, extra navigation.
- **UX**: Mobile-first design (70%+ traffic from ads). Fast loading. Clear visual hierarchy.
- **Ad Alignment**: Must align with "Salon suites Blaine", "Rent salon space near me".

## Architectural Constraints
- Form must be embedded and frictionless.
- Content must explicitly hit the 7 required sections smoothly.
