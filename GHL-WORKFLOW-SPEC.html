4. SMS copy to write (drafts for Rachel's approval)

Text #1 — Tour Confirmation (immediate): "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, it's {{manager_name}} at My Salon Suite {{location_name}}. Got your tour request! I'll reach out shortly to lock in your time. Talk soon."
Text #2 — Reminder (timed before tour): "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, quick reminder: your My Salon Suite {{location_name}} tour is {{appointment.start_time}}. We're at {{location_address}}. See you there! Reply if anything changes."
Text #2a — No-show / Cancelled: "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, sorry we missed you! No problem at all. Want to grab a new time? Book here: {{booking_link}}"
Text #2b — Toured, no reservation: "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, great meeting you at My Salon Suite {{location_name}}! Any questions about your suite or next steps? I'm here whenever you're ready to hold your spot."
Text #3 — Member check-in (1 week post move-in): "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, {{manager_name}} here. You've been in your suite a week! How is it going? Anything you need, just reply."
