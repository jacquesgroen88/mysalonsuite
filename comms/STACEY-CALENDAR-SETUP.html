Stacey: Connect Your Calendar (5 minutes)

Hi Stacey,

To make sure tour bookings land on your real availability and never double-book, we need to connect your Google calendar to the My Salon Suite leasing system. This takes about five minutes.

Before you start

You will receive an email invite to set up your login for the My Salon Suite leasing system. Click it, set a password, and log in. If you have not received it, let Rachel know.

Step 1: Connect your Google calendar

  1. Log in, then click your profile icon (top right) → Profile (or Settings → My Profile).
  2. Scroll to Calendar Configuration (also called "Connected Calendars").
  3. Click Connect next to Google, sign in with your work Google account, and allow access.

Step 2: Turn on two-way sync

Once connected, set both directions: - Check for conflicts: ON. This reads your Google calendar so prospects cannot book a time when you are already busy. - Add to calendar: choose your main Google calendar. This writes every new tour booking into your Google calendar automatically.

Step 3: Confirm your tour hours

  1. Go to Calendars and open "Blaine - Salon Suite Tour."
  2. Check the availability / office hours reflect the times you are able to give tours (for example, weekdays 9am to 5pm). Adjust if needed.

That is it

From then on: - Prospects only see times you are actually free. - Every booking appears on your Google calendar with the prospect's details. - The system sends the confirmation, reminder, and follow-up emails and texts automatically.

If anything looks off during setup, screenshot it and send it to Rachel. Thank you!
