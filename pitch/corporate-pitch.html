The Landing Page

A mobile-first, conversion-optimized landing page designed to align with paid search traffic ("salon suites near me", "rent salon space Blaine"). Built to convert a prospect within 10 seconds: clear headline, real suite photos, a visible "Book a Tour" form above the fold, and a full comparison table, before/after gallery, testimonials, and step-by-step process.

The Email System — Four Funnels

Funnel Emails What It Does
Initial Tour Sequence 5 01 to 05 Confirms tour requests instantly, sends preparation details, follows up with profit math, brand freedom, and community social proof to push a signed decision.
Guide Delivery Sequence 3 06 to 08 Delivers lead magnets (profit calculator, business guide), checks in softly, then escalates to a tour push for prospects who engaged but have not booked.
Evergreen Sequence 11 09 to 22 Handles every major objection over time: security, income, taxes, tech tools, client experience, hidden fees, branding, education, and more. Ends with a reactivation campaign for cold leads.
Member Resources Series 4 23 to 26 Three-part series introducing new members to business tools and savings, product partner pricing, and health and income protection benefits. Closes with a community welcome that establishes the Site Leader connection.

How It's Personalized

Nine Variables. Infinite Consistency.

The system is built around a small set of GHL contact field variables. Set these once per location and every email, every CTA, and every manager block populates automatically — no manual editing of individual emails.

Variable What It Controls
{{name}}Prospect's first name — appears in every email greeting
{{preferred_time}}Tour time window selected in the form — echoed back in Email 01
{{phone}}Prospect phone number — included in manager follow-up context
{{calendly_url}}Manager's Calendly booking link — drives the self-schedule CTA in Email 01
{{manager_name}}Manager's name — signs off on all personal-touch emails
{{manager_photo_url}}Manager's headshot URL — appears in the personalisation block in Email 01
{{manager_phone}}Manager's direct number — shown in Email 01's contact block
{{manager_email}}Manager's email address — shown in Email 01's contact block
{{facebook_group_url}}Location's member Facebook group — linked in the community email
What Rollout Looks Like

From Intake Form to Live System

Each location requires a single intake session. Once the inputs are collected, the build takes approximately two weeks from start to launch-ready.

1

Complete the Location Intake Form

The manager or operations contact fills out the standardized questionnaire: location details, manager profile, photos, community contacts, and GHL setup info. This is the only manual step required from the location team.

2

Asset Collection

Location photos (exterior, lobby, suites, hallway, lounge), manager headshot, Site Leader photo, and any before/after member transformations are gathered and optimized for web and email use.

3

Landing Page Build

The landing page is customized with the location's address, photos, manager info, and any location-specific details. The form is connected to the location's GHL sub-account.

4

Email Sequence Setup in GHL

All 26 emails are loaded into the location's GHL sub-account, GHL variables are mapped to the correct contact fields, and workflow triggers are configured. The manager is added as the sender identity.

5

Review and Sign-Off

The manager and operations contact review the landing page and email viewer side by side. Any location-specific adjustments are made before the system goes live.

6

Launch

The landing page goes live. Workflows are activated in GHL. From this point, every prospect who submits a form enters the automated nurture sequence with no manual intervention required.

The intake form is ready. The system is proven. The next step is selecting the first additional location and completing the questionnaire.
