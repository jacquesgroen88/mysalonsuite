When you're an employee at a commission salon or a basic booth renter, buying high-quality color, foils, and styling products just feels like watching your paycheck disappear.

But when you own your own salon suite, the math changes completely. 

Because you are a registered business owner, the supplies you buy for your clients become tax-deductible business expenses. 

That expensive specialized treatment you want to offer? Write it off.
The complimentary beverages you provide to your VIP clients? Write it off.
The cost of your suite rental itself? Write it off.

When you control the business structure, your overhead works for you at tax time, rather than just eating into your commission split.
